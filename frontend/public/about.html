About Raktify

Raktify is a free, non-profit blood donation platform built and operated by Choudhari EduHealth India Foundation, a Section 8 company registered in Amravati, Maharashtra. It is free for every donor, every camp organiser and every hospital and blood bank — and it will stay that way.

What Raktify does

For donors

Register once. We message you on WhatsApp only when your blood group is needed nearby, and you can stop the messages at any time.

Become a donor →

For camp organisers

Apply to host a camp for your college, company or society. Raktify partners a licensed blood bank, collects registrations and gives your camp a shareable page.

Host a camp →

For hospitals & blood banks

Raise and track blood requests, publish camp capacity a month ahead, record donations and screening results, and follow every bag from collection to transfusion.

How clinical decisions are governed

Raktify carries clinical reference data — donor eligibility rules, the blood-group compatibility matrix, component shelf life and transfusion-transmissible-infection deferral periods. None of it is our opinion.

  • That reference data was reviewed and signed off by an independent consultant haematologist on 10 July 2026, and is not changed except from a signed clinical source.
  • The rules live in the database as constraints and triggers rather than in application code, so an application bug cannot quietly bypass a clinical limit.
  • A self-reported blood group is never used to match a donor to a patient. Only a blood group verified by a licensed blood bank is, and only a blood bank can record that verification.
  • Nothing clinical is published in our Knowledge Center without a named reviewing clinician. That is enforced by the build itself, not by good intentions — an article marked clinical with no named reviewer will not publish. Our editorial policy sets out how we write and review it.

How we handle your data

Raktify asks donors for a mobile number, a blood group and a location, and asks institutions for clinical records. We treat all of it as something lent to us, not given.

  • No advertising, and no data sold or shared for marketing. There is no commercial model that would make that tempting — the platform is free and the Foundation is a non-profit.
  • Names, addresses and clinical notes are encrypted at rest. TTI screening results use a separate encryption key, so access to the general data does not grant access to screening data.
  • Every change to a donor or clinical record is written to an append-only audit log. No administrator can edit or delete an entry in it; there is deliberately no override.
  • Hospitals never see a donor’s mobile number. All contact between a hospital and a donor is mediated by the platform.
  • As a Data Fiduciary under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, we publish a named Grievance Officer in our privacy policy, and you can ask us to erase your data at any time.

How it is funded

Raktify is self-funded by the Foundation’s directors. It costs nothing to use and carries no advertising, which means it has no revenue of its own. If you would like it to reach further, you can support the Foundation — donations are made through give.do and carry an 80G receipt.

A note on what we do not claim. Raktify coordinates; it does not practise medicine. Whether someone may donate is decided by the medical officer at the blood bank on the day, after seeing them. Whether a unit is safe to transfuse is decided by the blood bank’s own screening and crossmatch. Nothing on this platform substitutes for either.
