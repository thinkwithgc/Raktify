About us

A free blood network
for all of India.

Raktify is a non-profit platform that connects voluntary blood donors with hospitals and blood banks across India. It is built and operated by Choudhari EduHealth India Foundation, a Section 8 company registered in Amravati, Maharashtra — and it is free for every donor, every camp organiser and every institution that uses it.

Clinical governance

None of the clinical rules are our opinion

Raktify carries donor eligibility rules, the blood-group compatibility matrix, component shelf life and transfusion-transmissible-infection deferral periods. Here is how each of those is kept honest.

  • That reference data was reviewed and signed off by an independent consultant haematologist on 10 July 2026, and is never changed except from a signed clinical source.
  • The rules live in the database as constraints and triggers rather than in application code, so an application bug cannot quietly bypass a clinical limit.
  • A self-reported blood group is never used to match a donor to a patient. Only a group verified by a licensed blood bank is — and only a blood bank can record that verification.
  • Nothing clinical appears in our Knowledge Center without a named reviewing clinician. That is enforced by the build, not by good intentions: an article marked clinical with no named reviewer will not publish. Our editorial policy sets out how we write and review it.

How we handle your data

We ask donors for a mobile number, a blood group and a location. We treat all of it as something lent to us, not given.

  • No advertising, and no data sold or shared for marketing. There is no commercial model that would make it tempting — the platform is free and the Foundation is a non-profit.
  • Separate keys for separate risks. Names, addresses and clinical notes are encrypted at rest, and TTI screening results use a different encryption key, so access to one does not grant access to the other.
  • An append-only audit log. Every change to a donor or clinical record is recorded, and no administrator can edit or delete an entry. There is deliberately no override.
  • Hospitals never see a donor’s mobile number. All contact between a hospital and a donor is mediated by the platform.
  • A named Grievance Officer. As a Data Fiduciary under India’s DPDP Act 2023 we publish one in our privacy policy.
  • Erasure on request. You can ask us to delete your data at any time, and we tell you what we must keep and why.

How it is funded

Raktify is self-funded by the Foundation’s directors. It costs nothing to use and carries no advertising, which means it has no revenue of its own. If you would like it to reach further, you can support the Foundation — donations go through give.do and carry an 80G receipt.

A note on what we do not claim. Raktify coordinates; it does not practise medicine. Whether someone may donate is decided by the medical officer at the blood bank on the day, after seeing them. Whether a unit is safe to transfuse is decided by the blood bank’s own screening and crossmatch. Nothing on this platform substitutes for either.
